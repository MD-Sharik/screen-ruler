Inches to Centimeters Conversion Chart (With Real-World Examples)

MD-Sharik August 28, 2026 3 min read

The inch-to-centimeter conversion is exact: 1 inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters, no rounding involved. That figure has been the internationally agreed conversion factor since 1959, when the US, UK, and several other English-speaking countries formally defined the inch in metric terms — the International Yard and Pound Agreement — specifically to standardize scientific and engineering measurement worldwide.

To get a more intuitive feel for what these measurements actually look like, it helps to compare them to familiar objects rather than just the raw numbers.

A quick note on the credit card row: a card's width, per the ISO/IEC 7810 standard used worldwide, is 85.60mm (3.37in) — it's the card's shorter height that lands close to 2 inches.
