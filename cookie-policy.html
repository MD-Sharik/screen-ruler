The ruler saves calibration in local storage. The website also uses existing Ezoic advertising and consent services. Optional Google Analytics can set separate cookies only after the consent manager provides explicit Analytics consent. It stays off when that consent cannot be confirmed.

Cookies vs. local storage

These two are often confused, so to be precise: a cookie is a small piece of data sent to a server with every request, typically used to identify a visitor across page loads. Local storage is different — it's a browser-only storage area that a page can read and write, but which is never automatically sent anywhere.

The ruler uses local storage to remember the pixels-per-millimeter value from your last screen calibration, so you don't have to redo it every visit. That value never leaves your browser.

Use the existing Ezoic consent manager to change or withdraw consent. Google Analytics does not load or send measurement requests where explicit consent cannot be confirmed. Use the persistent Analytics privacy control to read the Analytics notice. Existing Ezoic services have their own consent controls. See the Privacy Policy for data types and Google's privacy information.

Managing local storage

You can clear the stored calibration at any time through your browser's site settings (usually under "Site data" or "Storage" in your browser's privacy settings), or by using your browser's private/incognito mode, which discards it automatically when you close the window.
